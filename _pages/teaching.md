### 🛠 Workshops

#### Workshop on AI in Music Production  
**Years:** 2024, 2025  
**Venue:** The Rythmic Music Conservatory of Denmark (RMC)  
A yearly workshop on AI in music production. It includes a theoretical background, demonstrations of AI-based music production software, and reflections on the broader impact of AI on the music industry.

---

#### Workshop on Sound and AI (*Fra Stemmer til Spektrogrammer*)  
**Years:** 2023, 2024, 2025  
**Event:** Science Day at Aalborg University, Copenhagen  
A hands-on workshop for high-school students exploring sound in both physical and digital domains, and how AI can be used to manipulate and analyze sound.

---

#### Differentiable Digital Signal Processing (DDSP)  
**Year:** 2024  
**Course:** Signal Processing for Interactive Systems (SPIS), Aalborg University Copenhagen  
A guest lecture for the *Sound and Music Computing (SMC)* program. Introduced students to DDSP and its applications in modeling audio-processing systems such as guitar pedals and filters.
